# LIMEN Vault — System Home

Welcome to your independent, local-first Knowledge Vault.

## Structure
- **01_CLIENTS**: Client knowledge profiles & accounts
- **02_PROJECTS**: Project manifests & deliverables
- **03_BRANDS**: Brand identity & brand positioning specs
- **04_POSITIONING**: Strategic positioning frameworks
- **05_PACKAGING_KNOWLEDGE**: Packaging in Italy core domain knowledge
- **06_METHODS**: Methodologies & analytical workflows
- **07_CASE_STUDIES**: Completed case studies & success stories
- **08_MARKET_RESEARCH**: Market studies & sector intelligence
- **09_COMPETITORS**: Competitor analyses
- **10_APPROVED_OUTPUTS**: Human-approved final knowledge outputs
- **20_RAW_SOURCES**: Read-only raw source material (documents, transcripts)
- **80_AI_OUTPUTS**: AI generated responses & document drafts (AI Write Area)
- **90_PROPOSALS**: Candidate knowledge awaiting human approval (AI Write Area)
- **99_ARCHIVE**: Archived legacy pages
